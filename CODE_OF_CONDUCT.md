# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Spam or intentionally disruptive conduct

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who do not follow this code of conduct.
